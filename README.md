Agriculture is an important aspect in ensuring food security and the sustainability of the global population. However, plant diseases have been known to contribute to a significant reduction in agricultural productivity and economic losses for farmers. Conventional approaches to plant disease diagnosis and pesticide dosage application are manual and time-consuming. To overcome these challenges, this project proposes the use of an AI-based Drone System for Plant Disease Diagnosis and Pesticide Dosage Recommendation. Real-time identification of plant diseases and effective pesticide control are essential in contemporary agriculture. This paper
presents an autonomous drone precision agriculture system using deep learning, cloud computing, and IoT technology for plant disease prediction, smart pesticide selection, optimal dosage calculation, and precise spraying. The ESP32-CAM module on the drone takes highresolution images of leaves during autonomous flight and transfers them to Firebase Cloud Storage over Wi-Fi. The cloud-hosted Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) model analyzes the images to identify plant diseases and predict their severity levels with high accuracy.Depending on plant disease and severity percentage, the system automatically suggests the best pesticide and computes the optimal dosage based on pre-defined agronomic requirements.
